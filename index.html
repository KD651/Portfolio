BEFORE WE START

A few things
you might ask.

What does a website starting at US$300 include?+

A single page with the agreed sections, responsive design, content you provide, one revision round, and launch preparation. Domain, hosting, maintenance and additional features are separate. The final price is confirmed before work begins.

Can you add bookings or payments?+

We can connect your website to an existing platform, depending on your country and needs. The account and funds belong to your business. A custom booking or payment system requires a separate quote.

Will I be able to edit my website?+

Yes. You receive editable source files and a guide. These websites do not include a visual editing dashboard by default. If you need to edit content without code, we can plan for that from the start.

Are these projects for real clients?+

No. Mercer Barber and STILL are fictional brands created as portfolio concepts. They demonstrate design and interactions, not commercial results or client testimonials.
